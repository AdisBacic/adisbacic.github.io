Work

Shipped, and still running

Four products in production. Different domains, same standard.

Ideal Clinic

↗

The site for a medical aesthetic clinic with practices in Västerås and Stockholm, where every injectable is done by licensed staff. A treatment catalogue that has to read clearly to someone deciding whether to book.

DocLinguo

↗

Swedish, taught from your own paperwork. Upload the rental contract or the letter from school and an AI teacher called Maja builds the lesson out of the words you actually needed that week. Speech practice, spaced repetition, CEFR A1–C2 and TISUS prep.

Garnballer

↗

A crochet diary. Every row logged is written to the phone first, so it works in a tunnel with no signal at all — the cloud is a backup you press on purpose, never something the app waits for. Swedish, Turkish and Bosnian.

Summit

↗

A private stock club for one group of friends. Someone pitches a case, the crew votes, and a passing majority acts on it in a shared paper portfolio — then real market prices settle who was right. The link asks for an invite, so expect a closed door.

None of these existed until somebody refused to let the idea go. If you can describe it clearly, it can be built — the hard part was never the idea.

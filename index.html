What I work with

Money, and the truth about it

Three domains that all punish hand-waving in the same way.

01

Bookkeeping

Double-entry is unforgiving, and that is the entire point. Ledgers, reconciliation, VAT and audit trails that still hold up when somebody actually audits them.

02

Fintech

Payments, risk and compliance — the parts of a product where "it usually works" is not a passing grade, and where the edge case is the product.

03

Open banking

PSD2, AIS and PIS. Bank connections that behave themselves, and messy account data turned into something you can actually reason about.

Why the domain

There is no clean English word for it

"Stubbornness" is close. "Spite" is too mean. "Defiance" is too grand. Inat is what makes somebody rebuild a house brick by brick rather than let it be torn down — not because it is practical, but because it is theirs.

In financial software it turns out to be a useful trait. It is what makes you chase the one öre that will not reconcile at 23:40. It is refusing to ship a bank integration that works most of the time. Ledgers do not do "roughly".

  • No silent failures.
  • If it does not reconcile, it is not done.
  • Boring infrastructure, interesting products.
  • Write it down, or it did not happen.

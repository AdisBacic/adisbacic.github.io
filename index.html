Why the domain

There is no clean English word for it

inat

noun · Bosnian

  1. Stubbornness with a spine. Doing the thing precisely because you were told it could not be done.
  2. The refusal to accept "close enough" as an answer.

in use "He rebuilt it brick by brick, iz inata."

"Stubbornness" is close. "Spite" is too mean. "Defiance" is too grand. Inat is what makes somebody rebuild a house brick by brick rather than let it be torn down — not because it is practical, but because it is theirs.

Every project above started as a sentence somebody said in passing. Inat is what carries an idea past the point where it stops being fun: the fourth rewrite, the bug that only happens on someone else's phone, the week where nothing works. Skill decides what you can build. Inat decides what actually gets finished.

Day to day I work on financial software — bookkeeping, fintech and open banking.

  • Ideas are cheap. Finished is rare.
  • No silent failures.
  • If it needs signal to work, it does not work.
  • The fourth rewrite is where it gets good.
